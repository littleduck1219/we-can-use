# App Builders & Local LLMs

**English** | [한국어](app-builders-local-llm.ko.md)

Vibe coding platforms that build apps from prompts, plus tools for running open models on your own machine.

**Sections:** [Web App Builders](#web-app-builders-vibe-coding) · [Design to Code](#design-to-code) · [Local LLM Runtimes](#local-llm-runtimes) · [Model Hubs & Open-Weight Models](#model-hubs--open-weight-models)

## Web App Builders (Vibe Coding)

- **[v0 (Vercel)](https://v0.app)** — Generates full-stack web apps from prompts and deploys them instantly.
- **[Lovable](https://lovable.dev)** — Build apps and websites by chatting with AI, with one-click deployment.
- **[Bolt.new](https://bolt.new)** — StackBlitz's in-browser builder for websites, apps, and prototypes.
- **[Replit](https://replit.com)** — Cloud platform where Replit Agent builds and hosts apps from a description.
- **[Firebase Studio](https://firebase.studio)** — Google's full-stack AI workspace for frontend, backend, and mobile, powered by Gemini.
- **[Base44](https://base44.com)** — Full-stack apps with built-in DB, auth, and hosting from natural language (acquired by Wix).
- **[Emergent](https://emergent.sh)** — Handles coding, design, and deployment from an idea description.
- **[Create](https://www.create.xyz)** — Turns natural language into apps, sites, and tools with built-in AI features.

## Design to Code

- **[Figma Make](https://www.figma.com/make/)** — Create, refine, and deploy code-based prototypes from prompts.
- **[Google Stitch](https://stitch.withgoogle.com)** — Generates UI designs with AI and exports them to code.

## Local LLM Runtimes

- **[Ollama](https://ollama.com)** — The most popular LLM runner: open models locally (and in the cloud) with one command.
- **[LM Studio](https://lmstudio.ai)** — Desktop app for downloading and running open models through a GUI.
- **[llama.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp)** — C/C++ inference engine, the de facto standard of local inference (GGUF).
- **[vLLM](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm)** — High-throughput serving engine, the standard for production self-hosted inference.
- **[Jan](https://jan.ai)** — Open-source ChatGPT-alternative desktop app for local and online models.
- **[Open WebUI](https://github.com/open-webui/open-webui)** — Self-hosted web chat UI for Ollama and OpenAI-compatible APIs.
- **[LocalAI](https://localai.io)** — OpenAI API-compatible local runtime: text, audio, and vision, even without a GPU.
- **[AnythingLLM](https://anythingllm.com)** — All-in-one private AI app for document RAG and agents on your machine.
- **[GPT4All](https://www.nomic.ai/gpt4all)** — Nomic's privacy-focused desktop app; nothing leaves your machine.
- **[Text Generation WebUI](https://github.com/oobabooga/text-generation-webui)** — Local LLM UI supporting a wide range of backends (oobabooga).
- **[Msty](https://msty.ai/)** — Privacy-focused workspace for local and remote models in one interface.

## Model Hubs & Open-Weight Models

- **[Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co)** — The largest hub for open-weight models and datasets.
- **[Ollama Library](https://ollama.com/library)** — Open models ready to pull directly with Ollama.
- **[Llama (Meta)](https://huggingface.co/meta-llama)** — Meta's Llama model family.
- **[Qwen (Alibaba)](https://huggingface.co/Qwen)** — Alibaba Cloud's Qwen open-weight family.
- **[DeepSeek](https://huggingface.co/deepseek-ai)** — DeepSeek's open-weight models, including the V and R series.
- **[Mistral AI](https://huggingface.co/mistralai)** — Mistral's open-weight models.
- **[gpt-oss (OpenAI)](https://huggingface.co/openai/gpt-oss-120b)** — OpenAI's open-weight reasoning model gpt-oss-120b.
